How this view stays faithful to the model

The map uses unnormalized token lookup vectors (weights.wte), not position embeddings, attention weights, or the context-dependent representations produced later in the network. Axes are principal components, not named concepts.

Similarity uses cosine in the original vector space, from −1 (opposite directions) through 0 to +1 (same direction). It is not a probability or proof of shared meaning. *The highlighted neighbors use this full-space similarity, so they need not be the nearest dots in the compressed 3D map.

Movement lines join the recorded endpoints. They do not show the actual training path or intermediate steps. Before/after comparisons here apply to one run, not independently trained models whose coordinates may differ.

Open checkpoint.json from this nanoGPT lab to inspect your own run, including its recorded initial table. Legacy checkpoints without an initial table disable the comparison. Supports up to 512 tokens and 64 dimensions. Only the selected token and its closest neighbors are labeled to keep the map readable; select any dot or use the token menu.
